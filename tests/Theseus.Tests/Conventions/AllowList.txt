# What the Windows conventions decisions keep on purpose (baseline W5). This list stays; Exemptions/ is what shrinks.
# Rule | value | scope | reason
# scope: * for every file, glyph for a glyph anywhere, or a path under src/Theseus.App.
# value nudge: spacing whose only off-grid parts are a top or bottom of 1 or 2.

Colour | Transparent | * | Not a colour choice: nothing is drawn, so it is right in every theme and in high contrast.
FontSize | 16 | glyph | The glyph size in menus and the project-view toolbar (W22, checklist G41, G53).
FontSize | 8 | glyph | The drop-down caret, as WinUI's own DropDownButton draws it (W22, a recorded deviation in W4).
Spacing | nudge | * | A text-baseline nudge of 1 or 2 epx, vertical only (W36).
Spacing | -2 | Themes/Interactive.xaml | The keyboard-focus ring sits 2 epx outside its control, as the Fluent focus visual does.
Spacing | -3 | Themes/Interactive.xaml | A card's focus ring sits 3 epx outside the card's own border.
FontSize | 11 | Themes/CompactMenu.xaml | The accelerator text in Microsoft's copied MenuItem template (baseline section 5).
